About this prototype: Mira is the default original cartoon character. Upload a simple stick figure and use Trace sketch to estimate a pose, then drag Mira’s joint handles to refine it. The uploaded drawing is a reference layer; pose controls drive Mira’s limbs. Motion presets are procedural demos, not a trained AI model. Your files remain on your device.
