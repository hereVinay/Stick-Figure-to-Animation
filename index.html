About this prototype: Uploaded images appear as a reference layer; move the skeleton joints to align it. This prototype animates the editable skeleton, not the original image pixels. The sketch tracing and movements run directly in your browser. Movement descriptions currently use a small set of supported actions rather than a trained generative model. Your files remain on your device.
